Before You Replace It, Let Us Take a Look

Carpets, sofas and mattresses take a lot of daily wear. Over time, it starts to show:

  • Stains that keep coming back, no matter what you try
  • Dark, greasy patches on sofa arms and headrests
  • Pet hair and odours that never quite go away
  • Yellow marks and a tired look on your mattress
  • Flattened, dull carpet in hallways and on the stairs

Sound familiar?

Supermarket sprays and rented machines only reach the surface. Most of the dirt sits deep in the fibres, and the wrong product on the wrong fabric can make things worse.

Many items that look beyond saving just need the right treatment.

Our 4-Step Cleaning Process

The same careful method on every job, adapted to each fabric.

  1. 1

    Assess

    We check the material, the level of soiling and any stains, so we choose the right treatment and avoid damage.

  2. 2

    Pre-treat

    Specialist products go to work on dirt, body oils, stains and odours before the main clean.

  3. 3

    Agitate

    We work the solution into the fibres to loosen the dirt a quick clean leaves behind.

  4. 4

    Extract

    Professional extraction rinses the fibres and pulls the dirt and residue out.

You'll often see the difference during the clean itself. The full result shows once everything is dry.

FAQ

    

      
How long will it take to dry?
It depends on the material, how much cleaning the item needed and how well the room is ventilated. We'll give you an estimate for your job and aftercare advice before we leave.

      
Can you remove old stains?
Many stains improve a lot with the right treatment, but old or set-in stains need to be assessed first. Some will lighten rather than disappear completely. Send us a photo and we'll tell you honestly what to expect.

      
Can you deal with pet hair and odours?
Yes. We treat pet hair and odours as part of the clean, and for stronger smells there's a pet and odour treatment add-on from €20. Strong odours are assessed first, as results vary with the cause and how long it has been there.

      
Which areas do you cover?
We're a mobile service and come to your home in Dublin.

      
Is there a minimum charge?
Yes, there's a minimum call-out fee of €45.

      
Why is the final price confirmed after the assessment?
Every item is different. Size, fabric and condition change how much work is needed, so we confirm the price once we've seen the job, and always before any work begins.

      
Do you offer better rates for several items?
Yes. When you book two or more services on the same visit, you can get special multi-service rates. Mention everything you need when you ask for your quote.

      
What if I'm not happy with the result?
You're covered by our 48-hour satisfaction guarantee. If there's a problem related to our service, we'll assess it and, where applicable, re-clean the area at no extra cost, in line with our guarantee terms.
